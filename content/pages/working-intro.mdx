## What I do

### Set the technical direction

I advise engineering and data directors on tech and data strategies that translate business
needs into effective roadmaps their teams can actually execute.

### Design the right product

I work with product and business people to incrementally design easy-to-use digital products
that solve complex customer problems — bringing product, platform, and engineering thinking
so we build the right thing, not just build things right.

### Build and lead the teams that ship it

I lead [empowered](https://www.svpg.com/empowered-product-teams/), multi-disciplinary product
and platform teams (typically 4–8 engineers) to build software and data systems with modern
engineering tools and practices, fostering autonomy and ownership.

### Grow the next generation

I train, coach, and mentor engineers on modern delivery, AI-enabled software engineering, and
technical leadership — and I [share what I learn in public](https://medium.com/gitconnected/10-unconventional-lessons-from-10-years-working-as-a-software-engineer-873d5d4ae4a2),
contributing back to the engineering community.
