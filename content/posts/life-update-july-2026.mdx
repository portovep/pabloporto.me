**Location:** Galicia

I am back in Galicia after an amazing month working remotely in Cagliari, Sardinia. I had the pleasure of exploring several places in the area, including Alghero, and enjoyed a boat tour around the stunning _calas_ on the east coast. I can definitely say that Sardinia has the best beaches in Europe, although I should probably plan another trip to Greece soon to triple-check that claim.

Summer has always been my favourite time of the year. I usually spend it in my hometown in Galicia, reconnecting with family and friends. It is when I feel most grounded and give myself permission to slow down.

I am also in the middle of a social media detox after spending a few months trying to promote <Link href="https://wanderpass.app" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" className="text-link">Wanderpass</Link>, the travel app I am building, on Threads. I set myself the goal of growing my account to 1,000 followers mainly to experience firsthand how difficult distribution and marketing really are. Building products is only half the job. Learning how to get them in front of people is a skill I want to develop.

### Work

I got promoted to Principal Engineer last June, which feels like a significant milestone. I started at <Link href="https://www.thoughtworks.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" className="text-link">Thoughtworks</Link> in 2014 as a graduate fresh out of university. Over the last twelve years, I have steadily increased my impact while maintaining a healthy work-life balance. I wrote about the biggest lessons from that journey in <Link href="https://medium.com/gitconnected/10-unconventional-lessons-from-10-years-working-as-a-software-engineer-873d5d4ae4a2" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" className="text-link">this well-received Medium post</Link>.

Beyond that, I have been helping our clients modernize their data platforms so they can scale AI powered products and agents. I have also been experimenting with coding agents and <Link href="https://www.kaggle.com/whitepaper-the-new-SDLC-with-vibe-coding" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" className="text-link">how they are changing the software delivery lifecycle</Link>.

### Making

These days, I am deliberately focusing on one side project at a time. In the past, my attention was scattered across several pet projects. Wanderpass has my full attention now.

Over the last month, I revamped the <Link href="https://wanderpass.app" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" className="text-link">landing page</Link> and put more effort into distribution. The app is now approaching 50 users.

I am also working on bringing the app to native platforms and shipping the next major feature, which I hope will engage new users and increase engagement.

### Thinking

This month, I want to spend some time reflecting on what I want next across different areas of my life. I feel like big changes are coming, so my intention for the month is simple. **Slow down and reflect.**

I like feeling that I have a say in how I design my lifestyle. Disconnecting life's autopilot from time to time gives me that sense of perspective and control.

I am also spending time planning my next travel adventures. It looks like I will be taking a month of unpaid leave in Q3 to explore Australia and Indonesia. I might even do another surf camp so I can finally improve my surfing.
